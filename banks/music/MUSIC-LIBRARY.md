**Source order (OB 2026-11-07, ledger M58: "use real music first for short form and the viral ones that are in the bank before ever making it yourself, yt is a little different because of copyright rules so do copyright friendly and also make sure you match the tone"):**
- **Short form:** the viral songs saved here come first (`T7/MUSIC/`, Nineteen and the Chopin nocturne at the T7 root, any Lane B song with a file). Then the Lane A tracks, then a new real track whose page you've checked. Never music we make. Always ship a voice-only copy too; ads and boosted posts stay Lane A.
- **YouTube:** copyright-friendly only. That means a Lane A track whose page shows no "AI generated" and no "Content ID Registered", credited in the description. Never Lane B, an unknown source, or music we made.
- **Both:** match the video's tone (educational = upbeat; see the Vibe rule below). The QC gate's `bank` check FAILs music we made, Lane B or unknown songs on YouTube, and the flagged tracks below.

**Vibe rule (OB 2026-11-07, C4424: "I hate that tone and background music you chose. Oddly depressing for an educational video"):** educational videos get an upbeat, bright bed (major key, light, positive); never melancholic or moody. OB picks the bed by ear from 2-4 previews under his voice before the render, and no video reuses another's bed by default. Mixkit "Tapis" (Eugenio Mininni) and "Vastness" (Andrew Ev) are banned for educational videos.
