# Route: YouTube long-form to Instagram clips  (`yt-clips` in formats.json)

Use it when: OB says "clips from the YouTube video", "clip up the YouTube video", "reels from the long-form". Template T12. Also read: `subjects/graphics.md`, `subjects/qc.md`, `subjects/delivery.md`. Reviewer list: `~/ob-editor/qc/REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md`. This route bakes in no music and no sound effects (he adds his sound in the app); `subjects/audio.md` applies to the voice only.

## The bar
- **No clip of this kind has been approved yet.** "Way better than ob-moments" (OB 10-26/28) was the brief for the tool, not praise of a reel. The reference is rico.incarnati's "appetizer rule", measured in `~/ob-editor/styles/rico-appetizer-clips.md`: 11.2 cuts a minute, 248 words a minute, 43.5 motion events a minute, idle 10%, first move 0.03 s. Treat the first reels of a video as a test and run the whole gate on them.
- **The rule:** every clip stands on its own (one whole takeaway) and leaves them hungry. It ends on one of his own open-loop lines; the post caption names the YouTube video. No YouTube chip on screen (OB 10-28). OB 10-29: "make the reel clip from youtube alwasy under 1:30 mins, if it doesnt go well no need to clip, dont force it if its not good or a complete stand alone clip". Five picks is a ceiling, never a quota; a video with no standalone part gets no clip.
- **The look** is Dylan Page's receipts split (`~/ob-editor/styles/dylan-page-split.md`): a receipt on top for each claim he makes, him below, full-frame 9:16 face only on the telling lines. Built in `~/ob-editor/bin/ytclip_look.py`.

## Inputs
All of it must exist before starting. If any is missing, stop and name it; never cut from the YouTube export (its captions and edits are baked in).
1. The long-form edit's own folder (its WORK): the 4K camera concat the long-form was cut from, the 48 kHz voice master and the 16 kHz mic, the cut list (only the takes it kept, so no "cut that, Claude" tries), `events_resolved.json` (its B-roll, pages, photos, sourced data), `hf/assets/`. The voice master of C4372 is in `/Volumes/OB-ARCHIVE/OB-EDITS-WORKING/C4372-seed-oils-source/voice-synced/` (read only).
2. The YouTube video id and title (for the caption), and the video's retention curve from the `/youtube` skill's analytics. A moment where viewers drop is not a pick.
3. The data gate: the Content Lab RULEBOOK and NEXT-TESTS in the vault (`90-References/`, find by name with `ls`) say what has reach: defense and audit moments (about 1.3x reach), steps (1.18x), GLP-1 and supplement claims (shares), a number in the first line.
4. A sample `clipper.json` and `picks.json`: `/Volumes/T7/OB-EDITS/2026-10-28-yt-clipper/` (read only). Copy their shape.

## Steps
Run every `ytclip.py` command as `YTCLIP_NO_PUSH=1 python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ytclip.py ...`. Without the variable the engine adds a slow zoom to frame 1, which OB banned (11-07).
1. **Start.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py start yt-clips WORK --camera CAM.MP4 --mic MIC.wav --no-script "the long-form edit is the script" --ref https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ID`. Read what it prints. WORK is the job folder; render scratch is `~/ob-scratch/ytclip/`.
2. **`WORK/clipper.json`.** Fill the keys from the sample: `video`, `title`, `youtube_id`, `camera`, `voice48`, `mic16`, `longform`, `lut` (`~/ob-editor/luts/ob_grade_warmtan_hald.png`), `fps`, `split` per B-roll file. Set `airdrop` to `WORK/unused-airdrop`: the `deliver` stage is never run, `ob_job.py finish` delivers. → `WORK/clipper.json`.
3. **Read the whole video.** Transcribe with whisper large-v3-turbo, always `-l en`, and read the transcript start to finish with the retention curve beside it. → `WORK/plan/transcript.txt`.
4. **Pick.** List every stretch that passes all six tests: (a) one complete takeaway that needs nothing before it; (b) no "from the start of this video" or other pointer back; (c) ends on his own open-loop line; (d) every claim in it is true to the sources in the long-form's events (a misstated claim is out); (e) under the length ceiling (`max_s` in the route rules), aiming for 40 to 60 s; (f) he delivers it cleanly: eyes at camera, no stumbling. Score each against the data gate in input 3. Write them all, with the reason each passes or fails, to `WORK/plan/picks_candidates.json`, then the chosen ones (best first, at most five) to `WORK/picks.json` in the sample's shape: lines in play order, `text` = exactly what he says, `near` = the source second, title from his own words (first person, no "free", no dashes), post caption naming the video. If no stretch passes, the job ends here and the delivery note says there is no standalone clip. `ytclip.py words WORK A B` lists Apple's words for source seconds A to B.
5. **Cut.** `ytclip.py cut WORK [R1 ...]`. It builds each reel from the long-form's kept takes: Apple words per stretch, `zero_dead.cut_words`, `refine_edges`, the voice mastered with `voice_master`. The reel's first and last edges land on whole sentences inside a real breath; every other splice edge with his sound outside it moves to the nearest real pause. Two lines that meet in the source play as one stretch. When a line's sentence really starts earlier, add those words to the line's `text`. Read its checks: the word diff against `text` has 0 CUT-RISK words, silence is inside the gate's limit, and every edge passes the gate's edges test. → `WORK/cut.json` (`segs`) and the cut voice.
6. **Look-off.** `ytclip.py lookoff WORK [R1 ...]` finds every cut edge where his eyes are off camera. Cover each one with the previous receipt held in the split (him still below it), or move the cut to another take. Never a cutaway, a full-screen card or a text card (his face is on screen every frame he talks).
7. **Receipts.** One for each claim, from the long-form's events: video, photo, doc, count, bars, versus, study, quote. A document receipt is the page's own pixels, only the highlighted lines, never retyped by OCR (it misread "weapon" and "a drink" on 11-04). A receipt holds 2.5 to 6 s and ends at a clip, a turn line or the closer. If a claim has no receipt in the long-form's events, find the real thing (the study page, the product, his footage) and look at it before it goes in; no stock filler, no AI image, no text-only card. Save the sheet you looked at as `WORK/qc/sheets/<receipt id>.jpg` and list every receipt in `WORK/plan/plan_graphics.json`. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/qc_assets.py build WORK` and fill every row it lists (`subjects/graphics.md` section 8: which plan it reads, which fields); `viewed: true` only after you opened the sheet, saved as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. `qc_assets.py check WORK --route yt-clips` shows no FAIL. List the sheets in `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`.
8. **Frame 1.** Use `"frame1": "smallface"` for every reel: his own frame small under the two-line title, so he is on screen from the first frame. Do not use `noface` or `twoface`: they draw a dark card or a photo with no face. The first cut comes after 1.8 s. Look at the frame-1 still of every reel: the area around his box must be his own frosted studio, not black.
9. **Mock and breakdown.** `ytclip.py mock WORK [R1 ...]` makes a still per shot and frame 1 from the same compositor as the render; `ytclip.py page WORK OUT.html` makes the shot page. Look at every still. Write `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md` (picks and why, the receipts sheet, frame-1 stills, the stills). Hand it to a fresh `work-checker` agent and fix what it flags before the full render. Nobody waits for OB.
10. **Render.** `ytclip.py render WORK [R1 ...] --yield` renders the picture in parallel chunks and muxes the voice, behind every other render (clips are the lowest priority: OB 10-28 "this is the lowest priority so queue it behind the more important ones"). It runs the gate with every piece and the camera map (lip sync). Check `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/render_slots.py list` first.
11. **Look at the real output.** Make a 10 fps contact sheet of each finished reel (`ffmpeg -i REEL.mp4 -vf "fps=10,scale=216:-1,tile=10x8" WORK/qc/final-REEL-%02d.jpg`) and open all of it. Check: him on screen every frame he talks, no black, no text near his face, the receipt matches the line, captions are three words with the live word yellow, frame 1 is a finished picture, nothing zooms. Fix and re-render.
12. **QA.** `ytclip.py qa WORK [R1 ...]`: the words, silence, loudness, motion, no sound burst at 0 s, no text near his face. Ship only on PASS.

## Decisions that are yours
- **Framing.** Full frame is a 9:16 face crop from the 4K, centred on his face, no alternating scale (OB 11-07: "its zooming in and out non stop"; the old 108% alternation is dropped). The label sits under his chin, captions under the label. A horizontal clip plays in the top of the split at its own size over its own blur, never on a black fill.
- **Split geometry.** The seam, caption and credit positions are the engine's (`ytclip_look.py`): do not move them by hand. The source credit moves off any face in the footage.
- **Joins.** The line ends right after his last word; joins stay inside the route's join limit; no stretch without his voice longer than the route allows (the old silent prop beat before the first word is dropped, OB 11-04). Never cut a word (OB 10-28 twice: "stop cutting me off when im saying a word").
- **Music and sound.** None baked in. Do not run `music_pick.py` or `sfx_pick.py`; `ob_job.py start` marks bank and sfx not-applicable for this route.
- **Captions.** Three words, the live word #FFD400, Big Shoulders, never over his face. Titles and captions from his own words, first person, no YouTube-only wording on Instagram ("link in the description").
- **Copies.** One file per reel. No trial pack, no extra-size copy, no variant frame-1 versions (`versions` holds one entry).
- **Everything else** the picks, receipts and cut methods above decide. Never `ytclip.py deliver` (it writes into a delivery folder on the T7).

## Traps
- "im not on the screen at all" (OB 10-30, on reel R1): a full-frame card or no-face frame 1 while he talks. Stopped by steps 6 and 8 and the final sheet.
- Hand-typed receipt text (OCR misreads) and stock footage as a receipt: step 7 forbids both.
- Skipping the standalone test to make five reels: step 4 allows zero.
- Cutting a reel at 90 s or more: `ytclip.py cut` stops it; do not override.

## Finish
- Gate inputs: the script is the picks' `text` lines; pieces and camera map come from the render; `assets` record from step 7; `warn_answers.json` for each WARN with what you looked at. Route-required checks and records: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py status WORK`.
- `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py gate WORK REEL.mp4`, then a fresh `video-reviewer` on each reel with its picks lines, `BREAKDOWN.md`, the sheets, and the reference measurements above. Three rounds at most.
- `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py finish WORK REEL.mp4 --to "yt-clips R1 <slug>"` for each reel, one file each.
- Delivery note: the video id, each reel with its pick reason, which stretches were rejected and why, and the caption text per reel (also saved as `WORK/POST-NOTES.txt`). Monday review of how posted clips did: `ytclip.py review WORK`, see `subjects/after-posting.md`.
