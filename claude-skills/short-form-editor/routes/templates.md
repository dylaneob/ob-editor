Use it when: OB sends a script with a T-number, or names a format: Trainbloom (T17), Kallaway canvas (T06), GLP style / the level (T09 + Blender), Hormozi board (T22 to T24), Dylan Page split (T04, T18), follow pitch (T13), how-to (T14), Squat U fix (T16), Saraev screen card (T19), Nate Herk split list (T20), Sarah Snow essay (T21), Drew Canole split hook (T26). ## The bar
- **Approved, only one:** the GLP level test (OB 11-06: "That's what I'm fucking talking about let's go, way better, don't just use me though make it unisex") and the full edit at that level (posted 11-06). Look: a 3D object with the title, his b-roll cascading into the 2x2, a draining bar, 3D blocks counting to sourced numbers, word-pop captions, text behind his head once, something real moving every 1 to 1.5 s. Measured numbers and paths: `~/ob-editor/qc/gold.json` (format `reel-graphics`) and `GOLD-SET.md` section 2a. The kit that built it: `~/ob-editor/level-kit/README.md` and `lk.py`.
- **Every other template has no approved edit.** The bar is the creator's measured layout from the style card and the decode (25 to 30 of their reels at 10 fps). The first edit of a template is a test and still gets the whole gate and a reviewer.
- **Boring** (OB 11-06: "the graphics and animations and overlays are weak and boring, the GLP one is gonna flop it's boring as hell") means flat stills, 2D pictogram people, dark boards with a few words, captions that sit there.

## Steps
1. **Start.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py start reel-graphics WORK --camera CAM.MP4 --mic MIC.wav --script SCRIPT --template T17` (use the real tag). Read what it prints.
2. **Decode the creator, unless the style card was decoded from 25 or more reels at 10 fps and says so.** Run `/watch` on each reference reel, and for any move you cannot place run `~/ob-vendor/bin/rea-video decompose REF.mp4 -o WORK/rea --lang en`, then `review`, look at every shot sheet yourself, then `apply-review` and `summary` (never `--vlm`, `--env-file` or `remake-run`; its pixel-only "AI" verdict is not evidence). Make 10 fps contact sheets (`ffmpeg -i REF.mp4 -vf "fps=10,scale=216:-1,tile=10x8" WORK/decode/REF-%02d.jpg`) and look at all of them; a 2 s sheet is not a decode. → `WORK/decode/` sheets and `WORK/decode/NOTES.md` with the measured shares.
3. **Measure the creator's layout.** For 3 or more of their reels write the share of runtime for each layout (face full frame, full overlay, split, card, cutout over footage, source clip) and the cuts per minute, graphic events per minute, words per minute. → `WORK/decode/shares.json`.
4. **Write `WORK/qc/format_check.json`**: a list of rows, one per signature move of the creator: `{"what" (the move), "reference" (their measured value), "ours" (the planned value), "ok" (true when within about 10 points of their share, or the move is built as they do it), "note"}`. A move that cannot be built is not a row marked ok: build it (step 6) or the job stops and says which move. The gate fails a format_check with any row missing `ours`, `reference` or `ok`, or with `ok` not true. The creator's layout is the template (OB 11-06: "That isn't train bloom style").
5. **Cut his lines.** `takes.py` (last clean take of each line; the lines he shot stay in) then `zero_dead.cut_words`; every edge lands between words on a real pause; voice through `voice_master.py`; the mic only, never camera audio. Plans start every line on a whole sentence. → `WORK/plan/edl.json`, `WORK/audio/voice.wav`.
6. **Gather every picture, look at each, record it.** For each overlay, background, photo and clip: find the real thing the sentence names (his b-roll first, then his photos, a licensed clip or a screenshot of the actual page, a chart, a drawn object, or a Blender render). Open it. Reject a picture of something else, a stock filler, anything upscaled past the engine's limit, and every banned file (C1797 to C1802; his lean photo is DSC07329). Make a contact sheet of each chosen asset (`frame_sheet.py` for clips, ffmpeg `tile` for stills) and save it as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. The rows come from `qc_assets.py build` in step 8; `viewed: true` only after you opened the file, `matte: "birefnet"` for every cutout. A text-only overlay has no row that can pass.
7. **Cutouts: BiRefNet only.** `tmpl.py` and `brand_react_render.py` cut OB out with BiRefNet themselves (`~/ob-editor/bin/birefnet_cut.py`). Do not copy or patch the engine. The first pass takes about 1.5 min per second of footage on the CPU. After that the frames come from the cache: `WORK/matte-cache` when the plan sits in WORK, else `~/ob-scratch/matte-cache`. A stopped render resumes from it. The engine writes `WORK/qc/matte.json` and `qc_assets.py build WORK` marks those cutout rows birefnet. If the engine stops with `BiRefNet cut-out cannot run`, the venv, the model or onnxruntime is missing: do what its Fix part says. Never cut OB out another way. One cutout render at a time (`render_slots.py list` first). A matte outside an engine: `~/OpenMontage/.venv/bin/python -I ~/ob-editor/bin/matte_hq.py IN.mp4 OUT_DIR FPS`, or `IN.png OUT.png`. Look at 6 matted frames over the real background: no rim, no ghost hands, no blur beside him. → `WORK/qc/matte-sheet.jpg`.
8. **Plan the picture.** Fill the plan from the template card and the layout shares. Rules that hold inside every template: something real moves about every 1 to 1.5 s; numbers count, never sit still; springs only (damping from the route's rules); a picture lands before its word; text and captions in Big Shoulders with #FFD400 on the one word that matters; a background is the picture of that sentence (the exact anatomy, object, page or move), never random stock; no boxes or bubbles; nothing over his face; no black or near-black fill; no zooming (see Decisions). → `WORK/plan/plan.json`. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/qc_assets.py build WORK` and fill every row it lists (`subjects/graphics.md` section 8: which plan it reads, which fields); `viewed: true` only after you opened the sheet, saved as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. `qc_assets.py check WORK --route reel-graphics` shows no FAIL. → `WORK/qc/assets.json`.
9. **Sound.** Music: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/music_pick.py WORK --route reel-graphics --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav`, then `music_pick.py apply WORK` (→ `WORK/audio/manifest.json`). Sound effects: write `WORK/plan/moments.json` (`[{id, t, what}]`, one entry per graphic that lands), then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sfx_pick.py WORK --route reel-graphics`.  See `subjects/audio.md`. → `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`, `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json`.
10. **Breakdown, written and checked, not held.** Write `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`: the format check table, the shot list with one still per beat (`python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/tmpl.py still PLAN.json OUT.jpg T1 T2 ...`, or `lk.py stills PLAN t1 t2 ...`), the asset sheet and matte sheet names, the music pick and the sound plan. Look at every still. Hand BREAKDOWN.md and the stills to a fresh `work-checker` agent before the full render and fix what it flags.
11. **Render.** `render_slots.py list` first and wait for an open slot. T17 boards: `tmpl_board.py` element `tb`; Blender 3D: `board3d.py bars|tubes OUT ...` (writes `meta.json` with label positions) or `blender_insert.py OUT --text "14%" --look paper|dark`, rendered before the edit; a Blender render counts as a slot. `tmpl.py render PLAN.json OUT.mp4` records the pieces and camera map and runs the gate; the level kit renders with `lk.py render PLAN`. The mic is the voice, always.
12. **Look at the real output.** Make a 10 fps contact sheet of the finished file and open all of it. Check against the creator sheets and `format_check.json`: layout shares, cuts per minute, no static hold over 2 s, nothing over his face, no black, no scraps under 0.3 s, the cutout edge, every caption word on screen. Fix and re-render. → `WORK/qc/final-sheet-*.jpg`.

## Decisions that are yours
- **Face on screen while he talks.** Yes in every template except those full-overlay by design: T17 (OB 11-06: "train bloom style has full overlay not green screen of me": about half the runtime is full-screen overlays with his voice over them, always ends on his face) and T21 (OB 11-06: keep all of her style, his voice over his own b-roll). T19, T20, T22 to T24, T26 and the split and canvas formats keep him on screen every frame he talks (OB 11-10: "never not show me"); put the creator's screens, tiles and receipts in the top panel or card and him below, and record the creator's faceless share in `format_check.json` as a row with `ok: true` and the note "house rule M37, OB 11-10". No green-screen of him over a T17 board.
- **Zoom.** None. No push, drift or zoom-out, even where the creator uses one (T04 1.22 zoom-out, T21 and T25 pushes, T22 tight/wide alternation, the level brief's camera punches): OB 11-07 "its zooming in and out non stop and looks retarded" is newer than every card. A hard cut between two framings is allowed within the route's scale limit. List the dropped zoom in `format_check.json` as `ok: true`, note "OB 11-07".
- **Background.** Picture of the sentence. For Kallaway's canvas (T06) it is code-drawn crumpled paper that re-crumples (OB 11-04: "can you do the paper background that changes the rinckle over and over"): project copy on the T7, read only, `/Volumes/T7/OB-EDITS/2026-11-04-reactions/_work/paper_gen.py` (copy into WORK to run). For T22 to T24 and others without a stated one: his own studio from the same frame, frosted and warm.
- **Boxes, pauses, fonts.** Their boxes and boxed captions are out (OB 11-04): Big Shoulders on the picture, #FFD400 on one word. Their pauses are out. T21's serif captions (EB Garamond, `~/ob-editor/fonts/EBGaramond`) and T22's clean captions (Inter, `~/ob-editor/fonts/Inter`) stay: the creator's font is part of those formats and the card declares it.
- **Music and sound.** A bed on every edit from `music_pick.py`, even where the creator runs none (OB 11-06: every edit gets a music bed). The route's bed level wins over the creator's (Drew -11 dB, T21 -18 are dropped). Sound effects from the bank only, each tied to a graphic landing, at the route cap (Trainbloom's own rate is above it).
- **Unisex.** GLP and weight-loss visuals show men and women (OB 11-06 "make it unisex"), real footage of both.
- **Blender.** Wherever the format has a 3D object, bars or numbers (OB 11-06: "did you use blender and follow the creators we chose format wise"). Code-modelled or Poly Haven CC0 with a credit line, never an AI model or texture.

## Traps
- "That isn't train bloom style" (OB 11-06) and "seems you are horrific at finding there editing styles" (OB 11-07). Stopped by steps 2 to 4: no layout is built before the shares are written.
- "You didn't do the green screen well", "You put blur next to me" (OB 11-09). Stopped by step 7: BiRefNet, sheet of matted frames, no blur boxes anywhere beside him, board writing left as shot.
- "They use really good background for their greenscreens. Not just random ass pixel photos" (OB 11-06 night). Stopped by step 6: a background is the exact object, anatomy or page, at full quality.
- "I hate your color grading honestly, no color at all it seems" (OB 11-09). Grade by measurement on his frame (`sync_av.py`, warm tan default), skin never darker.

## Finish
- Gate inputs: `format_check` and `assets` records, `music` and `sfx` records, the script, recorded pieces and camera map (the engine writes them), `warn_answers.json` for every WARN.
- `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py gate WORK FILE`, fix every FAIL, then hand the file to the `video-reviewer` agent with: the script, `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`, `format_check.json`, the creator's sheets and the approved exemplar for the format in `~/ob-editor/qc/gold.json`. Fix what it flags (three rounds at most).
- `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py finish WORK FILE --to "reel-graphics T17 <slug>"`. One file. The voice and sound stem stays in WORK so the bed can be swapped.
- Delivery note (`subjects/delivery.md`): the template, the creator, the biggest format-check rows, the music pick with runner-ups, any factual conflict as one clause.
