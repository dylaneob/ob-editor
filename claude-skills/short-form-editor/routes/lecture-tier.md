# Route: Lecture at the TV, tier list, this-or-that  (`reel-lecture` in formats.json)

Use it when: OB filmed himself at the TV with slides ("lecture", T10, the That Zona Guy / Kara format), or ranked things on the tier board or ran an "X vs Y" this-or-that (T11, the Dan Martell tier board). Also read: `subjects/audio.md`, `subjects/graphics.md`, `subjects/qc.md`, `subjects/delivery.md`.

## The bar
- **No approved edit exists for either format.** OB has not praised a finished lecture or tier edit (GOLD-SET section 3). Treat the first one of a kind as a test, and hold it to the numbers the gate prints for the route plus the common bar of every approved file: -14 LUFS, true peak inside the master limits, lip sync inside 10 ms, no retake cues.
- **Creator references:** lecture, That Zona Guy / Kara, plus the two upgrades in `~/ob-editor/styles/TEMPLATE-CATALOG.md` (T10: the Kallaway returning map, where the lecture's model goes on the TV slide once and comes back per point; Mekhi's step header for numbered points). Tier list (T11 upgrades: the ranking hook shape "Rating [X] so you don't waste your [Y]", and the S-tier reveal held to the end; notes in `/Volumes/T7/OB-EDITS/C4079-C4080/TIER-NOTES.md`): Dan Martell's tier board, Instagram post DdO1aQtCPGG; this-or-that: OB's reference clips in `/Volumes/T7/OB-EDITS/references/this-or-that-2026-10-16/` (reading the T7 is fine).
- **His rules for both:** the TV is his slide, so a card never repeats what the TV already shows big and clear; cards go where the TV is small, wrong or unreadable. Something real moves; no text-only card.

## Inputs
- Camera file (4K, the TV and him in frame) and the mic. Look for the MOTIV recording first (`My_Activity_<date>_<time>/MainOutput_24bit.wav` on the T7). Pair it with the take by duration; where the camera clock disagrees, the camera `creation_time` minus about 3 h 40 m (the Sony clock offset seen 10-15) plus a duration match finds it. If no mic file exists for that take, stop and say so: camera audio is never the voice.
- A script or plan. Lecture: whatever he said at the TV, written to `WORK/script.txt` one sentence per line from the transcript. Tier / this-or-that: the plan rows (`src` [a, b], `say`, events pop, place, win, tag, cta).
- The 16 kHz analysis copy of the mic for the QC tools: `ffmpeg -nostdin -i WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav -ar 16000 -ac 1 WORK/audio/take_16k.wav`. It is for measuring only; the voice always comes from the 48 kHz mic.

## Steps
Start: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py start reel-lecture WORK --camera CAM.MP4 --mic MIC.wav --script SCRIPT` and read what it prints.

**Both formats, first:**
1. **Sync and words.** `sync_av.py CAM.MP4 MIC.wav --measure-only`, then `--audio-only WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav` (refuses a residual over 15 ms). `~/ob-editor/bin/speech_words words WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav > WORK/words.json`.
2. **Bad takes out, zero dead space, the voice.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/takes.py WORK/words.json WORK/script.txt --audio WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav --fps 29.97 --edl WORK/edl.json --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav --master`. A try that "cut that, Claude" follows, one that stops short and a restart are dropped; the last clean try stays; lines he shot stay in. Every pause of 70 ms or more between words is out, never a speed-up, never a cut inside a word. The chunk and "islands" methods are not used for the cut; the QC tools below only check it. → `WORK/edl.json`, `WORK/audio/voice.wav`.
3. **Prove it,** each result saved to WORK/qc: `takes.py ... --verify > verify.txt` (0 missing or clipped words), `zero_dead.py measure WORK/audio/voice.wav > measure.txt` (silence and the longest gap inside the route's limits), `voice_master.py check WORK/audio/voice.wav > voice_check.txt`, `zero_dead.py lookoff CAM.MP4 WORK/pieces.json > lookoff.txt` (`pieces.json` as in `routes/short-form.md` step 4) (no piece opens or closes on him looking away; fix with another clean take, else a cut-away, else the next pause; never into a word).
4. **Grade the TV neutral.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/make_grade.py --help` lists the warm, white-balance and contrast controls. Build the warm-skin grade OB chose on the first batch (his words, 10-16: "warm skin", the one of three he picked): warmth fades toward white so the TV stays neutral. A plain warm LUT turned the TV pink. Verify by measurement on a graded still: the TV's white region reads neutral (R, G and B within a few levels) and skin keeps its colour. Make the still with `lstill.sh TAKE T CROP|none LUT OUT` (row 0 at `src_in` plus 0.1 s so frame 1 is the real first frame). → `WORK/graphics/grade_check.jpg` and the numbers in `WORK/notes.md`.
5. **Scan every used shot for Mac notifications mirrored on the TV** (frame sheet every 1 s of each used range, `frame_sheet.py CAM.MP4 WORK/qc/tv_scan.jpg T T ...`, and look). A shot with a notification is cut or patched.
6. **A typo or wrong number on the TV:** `tv_patch.py build|still|band` (use `~/OpenMontage/.venv/bin/python`) re-sets the slide's own letters; patch clips must use an explicit BT.709 round trip. Check a 3x contrast stretch and a diff map: only letters may change. A wrong number he says aloud stays in the voice; the card or patch shows the correct figure and the delivery clause names it.

**Lecture (T10):**
7. **Rows and cards.** Build the lecture EDL JSON (`items` and `rows`; the format is documented at the top of `lecture_cards.py`) with one row per piece of `WORK/edl.json`, source times from the pieces, one framing for every row (no alternating crop; no zoom of any kind). Cue per item: `card` (item, big, table, list, checks, title, close; `\n` breaks a line), `stamp` (BANNED, CAP, NOT NEW), `hide`. Layout, fixed: frame 1 shows the hook block (TODAY'S LECTURE kicker plus the hook lines) in the card zone below his face until the first card, the TV title readable above it; no captions and no duplicate title card under it; no number in the hook ("THE LAST ONE", not "#7"). Cards go over his lap (top 1040). Captions go on the black shirt (top 845), three words, live word yellow. Stamps sit at the card's top-right corner. Type is Big Shoulders Display, yellow on the one word that matters, no boxes. Anything visible on frame 1 must be visible by its own CSS (a GSAP `tl.set(..., 0)` does not render frame 0).
8. **Mock stills.** `lecture_cards.py EDL hf NAME --mock`, then `node lecture_mocks.js EDL hf NAME`. → `WORK/graphics/` stills; contact sheets saved and listed in `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`.
9. **Lecture QC at zero.** `lecture_qc.py EDL WORK/audio/take_16k.wav CHUNKS.json --fix` (soft endings and onsets outside a cut, speech leaking into a gap; `chunks.py WORK/audio/take_16k.wav WORK/chunks.json` makes CHUNKS.json) and `lecture_asr_qc.py EDL WORK/audio/take_16k.wav` (it transcribes each kept line and a 1 s window of the source and flags extra words not separated by a 120 ms pause). Both must read 0 before the breakdown. → `WORK/qc/lecture_qc.txt`, `lecture_asr_qc.txt`.
10. **Breakdown.** `lecture_breakdown.py CONFIG.json WORK/review/breakdown.html` for the sheet; `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md` lists every row (time, line, card or stamp, TV content, sound event, music section). Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/qc_assets.py build WORK` and fill every row it lists (`subjects/graphics.md` section 8: which plan it reads, which fields); `viewed: true` only after you opened the sheet, saved as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. `qc_assets.py check WORK --route reel-lecture` shows no FAIL. A fresh `work-checker` agent reads both plus the script and must say PASS before the full render (three rounds).

**Tier list / this-or-that (T11):**
7t. **Plan.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/tier_edl.py islands WORK/audio/take_16k.wav WORK/islands.json` gives speech runs split at the tool's pause length, each transcribed alone (Whisper full-take word times drifted up to 1.3 s on these takes, so plan against island times). Write `plan/NAME_plan.json` (rows `src` [a, b], `say`, events pop, place, win, tag, cta) using the `WORK/edl.json` pieces for the cut points, then `tier_edl.py build PLAN WORK/audio/take_16k.wav WORK/NAME_edl.json`. Edges snap to energy and touching rows join. A gap that would leak speech is closed or filled with room tone, never longer than the route's silence limit. The board kind is `tier` (S to F rows; items pop over the chest and spring to slots; a long row reflows to two lines) when he ranks, `thisorthat` (champion card left, challenger right, green check on the pick) when he compares two.
8t. **QC.** `voice_track_qc.py` and `tight_asr_qc.py` (transcribe each side of every mid-sentence cut on its own). Both clean. → `WORK/qc/`.
9t. **Mocks.** `tier_cards.py EDL hf NAME --mock`, then `~/OpenMontage/.venv/bin/python tier_stills.py EDL hf NAME LUT` (YuNet takes the topmost face, eyes at y 548; the dog gets detected otherwise), `tier_cards.py` again, `node tier_mocks.js hf NAME`. Review page: `tier_review_page.py WORK WORK/review/tier.html`. `BREAKDOWN.md` as above; `work-checker` PASS before rendering.
10t. **Order of the list.** The order is the script's. The hook is whatever he says first; the editor does not reorder lines. OB's scripting rule (10-16) is that the most controversial item opens and the second most follows; if the script does not do that, the delivery note says so in one clause.

**Render both (picture only):**
11. **Music and sound.** `music_pick.py WORK --route reel-lecture --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav`, then `apply WORK` (at least three scored candidates), and `sfx_pick.py WORK --route reel-lecture` from `WORK/plan/moments.json` (`[{id, t, what}]`), per `subjects/audio.md` (a card landing, a stamp hit, a row tick, a whoosh on the hook clearing; each tied to its event id). → `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`, `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json`.
12. **Render the picture.** After `render_slots.py list` shows a free slot: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/lecture_render.py WORK NAME` (writes `WORK/out/NAME_A.mp4`; `--flash` builds the V version with flash rows only when he asked for it) or `tier_render.py WORK NAME --lut LUT` (`--rows N` makes a short test; set `OB_HF_WORKERS` to what `render_slots.py` allows). Each engine builds its own audio with its own kit and master: discard it. Put the finished mix on the picture: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/music_pick.py mix WORK WORK/out/NAME_A.mp4 --out WORK/out/final.mp4` (voice, bed and planned sounds, mastered, picture copied, `aac_at`, pieces carried over). The gate and the reviewer run on `final.mp4`. → `WORK/out/final.mp4`.
13. **Look at the real output.** `lecture_render_qc.py OUT EDL SHEET` or `tier_render_qc.py WORK NAME OUT SHEET` (black and frozen frames, loudness, a sheet of every graphic moment), then your own contact sheet of the final every 0.5 s for the first 3 s and every 2 s after, `WORK/qc/final_sheet.jpg`. Compare with the mock stills. Check frame 1 (hook block or board visible), captions off his mouth, TV never covered where it is readable, lips against sound at start, middle and end.

## Decisions that are yours
- **Camera or mic audio:** the mic, always.
- **Cut method:** `takes.py` (step 2); the chunk and island tools only check.
- **Framing:** one framing per reel; no alternating 108% rows.
- **Grade:** the warm-skin, TV-neutral grade (step 4). No menu.
- **Board kind:** `tier` for a ranking, `thisorthat` for two things.
- **A card that repeats a big readable TV line:** dropped.
- **Version V (flash):** only when he asks.

## Traps
- **Cut mid-word.** Chunk cuts clipped "It's" in "It's not the protein" and "it" in "eating it" (first 10-15 cut). Steps 3 and 9 at 0.
- **Pink TV** from a plain warm LUT. Step 4 measures the TV white.
- **TV notifications** captured in the shot (C4349). Step 5.
- **Frame 1 empty because a timeline `set` at 0 does not render frame 0.** Step 7.
- **Code-kit sound and its own loudness** from the engines. Step 12 discards it.
- **Not on screen:** he is on screen every frame he talks; the board and cards sit on his chest and lap, never instead of him (OB 10-30, again 11-10: "never not show me").
- **Dog detected as a face** in tier stills. Topmost face, eyes at y 548.
- **Seam in a patched TV strip** (2.4 levels darker). The BT.709 round trip in `tv_patch.py`.

## Finish
Gate inputs: camera, mic, script, `WORK/pieces.json`, `music_candidates.json`, `sfx_plan.json`, `WORK/qc/assets.json`, `warn_answers.json`. No recorded pieces? `subjects/qc.md`, "When the gate says there are no recorded pieces". `ob_job.py gate WORK WORK/out/final.mp4`; the reviewer gets the file, script, `BREAKDOWN.md` and, since no gold exemplar exists, the line "first edit of this format"; three rounds at most; then `ob_job.py finish WORK WORK/out/final.mp4 --to "what it is"`. The note names the music and runner-ups and any one-clause factual conflict.
