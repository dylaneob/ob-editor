Use it when: OB sends raw takes of a numbered list ("10/10", "10 out of 10", a 3 to 6 item plan), says "the Megann plan", "the 2x2" or "4x4", or "upgrade the 10/10 trial winner". Also read: `subjects/audio.md`, `subjects/graphics.md`, `subjects/proof-clients.md`, `subjects/qc.md`, `subjects/delivery.md`.

## Inputs
- Camera file, MOTIV mic WAV, the script with its item numbers and hook lines. The structure is the script's: hook lines first, then the items in script order. Do not reorder lines. (OB's scripting rule, 10-16: open on the most controversial item, then the second most; that is decided when the script is written.)
- The posted or trial version of this video, if one exists: its look decides step 2, and its file is the "previous cut" for the damage check in step 5. Find it in the delivered folder named by `ob_job.py status`, or from the post link he gave.
- Photos: his own footage and real photos only (see Traps). The style card for the chosen look, read whole.

## Steps
Start: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py start reel-lists WORK --camera CAM.MP4 --mic MIC.wav --script SCRIPT`. Read what it prints.

1. **Sync and words.** Same as the talking route: `sync_av.py CAM.MP4 MIC.wav --measure-only`, then `--audio-only WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav` (a take recorded during a render needs `sync_steps.py`); `~/ob-editor/bin/speech_words words WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav > WORK/words.json`; script to `WORK/script.txt`. Warm-tan LUT `~/ob-editor/luts/ob_grade_warmtan_hald.png` in the plan; never grade A. → `WORK/words.json`.
2. **Choose the look by this rule, in order,** and write it as `look:` in `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`: (a) OB named one: "the Megann version" is MX for that video only; "2x2" or "4x4" is the grid; "Kyle" is the panel. (b) A trial winner or posted version of this video exists: its look (for the three 10/10 feeds that is the 2x2 grid). (c) Otherwise the 2x2 grid with variant A of the style card (a 2x2 of four photos per item, his cut-out across the seam the whole way), because grid openers beat talking-head openers on his own data. A reference copy he asks for is for that one video, never the new feed default (memory `feedback_feed_winner_upgrade_only`).
3. **Cut.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/takes.py WORK/words.json WORK/script.txt --audio WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav --fps 29.97 --edl WORK/edl.json --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav --master`. Bad takes out (the last clean try of every line stays; REVIEW lines get the most complete try); every pause of 70 ms or more between words out; never a tempo change; the voice from the MOTIV mic through `voice_master`, never hard-clipped. Detail and reasons: `subjects/audio.md`. OB 10-28 on the old 10/10 trials: "they were pretty shit tbh", "a lot of pauses", "and deadspace".
   - Each item number gets a 0.08 s beat before it so "Four" is not clipped.
   - Every edge takes in sound still rising into or fading out of its word (within 44 dB of the line; at most 60 ms before and 80 ms after; never past the neighbouring word). Without it the cut clipped the "f" of "Four" and the end of "bare minimum".
   → `WORK/edl.json`, `WORK/audio/voice.wav`.
4. **Prove the cut.** Each saved to WORK/qc: `takes.py ... --verify > WORK/qc/verify.txt` (0 missing or clipped words); `zero_dead.py measure WORK/audio/voice.wav > WORK/qc/measure.txt` (silence and the longest gap inside the route's limits); `voice_master.py check WORK/audio/voice.wav > WORK/qc/voice_check.txt` (no flat peaks, loudness and true peak inside the master limits); `zero_dead.py lookoff CAM.MP4 WORK/pieces.json > WORK/qc/lookoff.txt` (`pieces.json` as in `routes/short-form.md` step 4) (no piece opens or closes on him looking at his notes; in the GUT take 5 of 12 edges did). Fix a lookoff flag: another clean take, else a cut-away over the edge, else the next pause; never into a word.
5. **Damage check against the previous cut,** when a trial or posted version exists: transcribe the new voice and the old voice with Apple (`speech_words words`) and whisper medium (`--verify` for the new one), then diff word for word. A word the old cut kept and the new one lost is damage; fix the edge. A word lost in both readings, or mid-line where nothing was cut, is the ASR. → `WORK/qc/diff_old_new.txt`.
6. **Photos.** One photo set per item, chosen so the photos argue the line (the topic's own objects, then what it really costs or does), not generic athletes. Every image on a contact sheet first (`python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/frame_sheet.py MEDIA WORK/qc/sheets/ROW_ID.jpg T ...` for clips; stills are looked at in the sheet the plan stills produce) and listed as an `els` entry of the step 7 plan with `kind`, `source`. The source URL of every image is checked before it goes in: no AI source (StockCake, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Midjourney, Pixabay items labelled AI). No upscale past the route's limit. Pictures are on screen from 0.0 of the hook and land just before the word they argue. Men and women both (OB 11-06: "make it unisex"). Cutouts: BiRefNet only (`matte_hq.py`). His own photos: the banked retouched set, rotated (OB 10-30: "Don't do the same photos for me over and over mix them up"), never C1797 to C1802; his lean photo is DSC07329.
7. **Plan.** Write the `tmpl.py` plan (`python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/tmpl.py --help` prints the schema). The grid is the `grid` look (the engine cuts OB out with BiRefNet itself: `routes/templates.md` step 7): `cells` (two rows of four paths), `swap` (the word or time at which the cells cascade clockwise), `face_w`, `face_top`, `clip`. Item numbers come from the `itemno` element, the one device (laptop screen, notes) from `slide`. The grid cascades to its contrast set when the hook pivots ("instead of ..."). The cutout is about twice the corner-inset size, holds the hero prop and is paid off in the last 5 s. For an essay body, backgrounds swap on concrete nouns behind his cutout. The twelve upgrades are in `two-by-two-grid.md`; take every one that fits the script, and log the ones you left out with a reason in `BREAKDOWN.md`. The engine copy on the T7 (`/Volumes/T7/OB-EDITS/2026-10-24-tenten/_work/tenten3_c4376_edit.py`, project copy, read only: copy it into WORK to change it) is a geometry reference only; do not run it, because it predates the zoom, cutout, sound and music rules. Captions: his words, three at a time (Megann: one word at a time), brand type, below his chin, no box.
8. **Stills and breakdown.** `tmpl.py still WORK/plan.json WORK/stills.jpg 0.1 T T T`: frame 1, every item's first frame, the pivot, the last second. Write `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md` (look, per shot: time, line, pictures with source, overlay kind, sound event, music section). Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/qc_assets.py build WORK` and fill every row it lists (`subjects/graphics.md` section 8: which plan it reads, which fields); `viewed: true` only after you opened the sheet, saved as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. `qc_assets.py check WORK --route reel-lists` shows no FAIL. A fresh `work-checker` agent reads it with the script and stills and must say PASS before the full render; fix and re-check, three rounds.
9. **Music and sound.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/music_pick.py WORK --route reel-lists --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav`, then `music_pick.py apply WORK` (at least three scored candidates; the winner and runner-ups are named at delivery); `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sfx_pick.py WORK --route reel-lists` for sound tied to what lands on screen, under the route's cap, none on a photo that simply holds (Megann: no sound on overlays). Both per `subjects/audio.md`. → `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`, `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json`. The old feeds were voice-only (OB 10-29: "do no background music i will find a good version on my own"), but OB 11-06 and 11-07 set a bed on every edit and "real music first"; the voice-plus-sound stem stays in WORK so he can swap the bed in a minute.
10. **Render** when `render_slots.py list` shows a free slot: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/tmpl.py render WORK/plan.json WORK/out/render.mp4`. Contact sheet of the render (every 0.5 s for the first 3 s, then every 2 s) saved to `WORK/qc/final_sheet.jpg` and compared with the stills: pictures there at 0.0, grid never empty, nothing over his face, captions off his mouth.
11. **Gate, reviewer, finish:** see Finish.

## Decisions that are yours
- **Look:** step 2's rule. No menu.
- **How many items get a full photo set:** every item; the micros version ran a 2x2 of four photos per item.
- **Hook pivot:** the grid cascades if the hook has a contrast; static if it has none.
- **Music or voice only:** a bed, per `subjects/audio.md` (see step 9).
- **Zoom:** none. The old 10/10 engine alternated 100 and 108% framing; do not.
- **A numbered claim that conflicts with the evidence:** the line stays; one clause in the delivery note.

## Traps
- **The Megann copy on a feed 10/10 was hated** (found 10-29). Megann look only when he says it, for that video.
- **Mock from the posted render** inherited its cut-offs (M27, OB 10-28: "you still fucking cut me off on fdoe"). Cut from the raw take.
- **AI photos** in posted 4x4 trials (10-28, StockCake and ChatGPT). Step 6 checks every source URL.
- **Photos flashing in** (OB 10-29). On screen from 0.0.
- **Bad background music** (OB 10-28: "bad background music choice"; the track later turned out to be AI-labelled). Only banked tracks with a clean licence record, via `music_pick.py`.
- **Photos of him repeated or unflattering** (OB 10-28: "horrible photos of me"). Rotate; look first.
- **Weak opening grid** that sat still for 3 s. Something moves inside 1 s.
- **Grading too pink or albino.** Warm tan only.

Gate inputs for this route: camera, mic, script, `WORK/pieces.json`, `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`, `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json`, `WORK/qc/assets.json`, `warn_answers.json`. Run `ob_job.py gate WORK WORK/out/render.mp4`, give the `video-reviewer` the file, script, `BREAKDOWN.md`, the previous version if any and the two exemplar names, fix what it flags (three rounds at most), then `ob_job.py finish WORK WORK/out/render.mp4 --to "what it is"`. One file per video: the trial winner's look, built from the raw take, no variant pack (OB 10-28: "Just remember I'm only post 1 of each to feed we already did trials"). The delivery note names the look, the music and its runner-ups, and any one-clause factual conflict.
