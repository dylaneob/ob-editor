# Route: YouTube long form  (`long-form` in formats.json)

Use it when: OB sends a camera file plus a mic WAV, or says "new youtube video", "edit it like Dan Go", "like Markus", "like Johnny Harris", "like Nippard". Teaching, documentary and long reactions. Reels cut from a YouTube video are `yt-clips`.
Also read: `subjects/audio.md`, `subjects/graphics.md`, `subjects/proof-clients.md`, `subjects/qc.md`, `subjects/delivery.md`; after he posts, `subjects/after-posting.md`. Titles, thumbnails: `references/youtube-thumbnails.md` and `/youtube`.

## The bar
- **Exemplar C4372 "We Lost the Plot" v3** (41:36; OB 10-23: "amazing job"). Measured: 24.3 visual changes a minute, median shot 1.94 s, his face 52%, any graphic 88%, longest face-only stretch outside intro and CTA 7.4 s. It landed on: the title's promise as a picture beside his face from 0:02; real clips with sound early, sentences finished; no roadmap, no repeats; a client shown whenever he named one; a client run to close.
- **Graphics bar, the C4398 kinetic test** (OB 10-30: "i like the bigger text an effects so much better"): Big Shoulders Display Black 470 to 640 px, words rising from a mask on the spoken word, key-word slam with a brush stroke, charts that count up, blurred warm A-roll behind panels.
- **Creators:** Dan Go and Markus Hunter (about 6.5 overlays a minute, median overlay 3.5 s); Johnny Harris for documentary (`~/ob-editor/styles/johnny-harris.md`); Jeff Nippard for graphics.

## Inputs
Camera file (C####.MP4, 4K), mic WAV (`MainOutput_*.wav`), script if any, the named reference, the title and thumbnail idea. The camera's own audio is never in the edit. Missing input: stop, say which.

## Steps
Each step ends in the file named. `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py status WORK` shows what is missing. The per-video scripts (`c####_cut.py`, `make_spec.py`, `prep.sh`, `build.sh`, `story/*.py`) come from the newest finished long-form WORK: project copy on OB-ARCHIVE `/Volumes/OB-ARCHIVE/OB-EDITS/C4465-edit/WORK` (read only: copy into your WORK to change). Steps 12 and 13 replace its music and sound-effect scripts.

1. **Start.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py start long-form WORK --camera CAM.MP4 --mic MIC.wav --script SCRIPT` (or `--no-script "why"`). → `WORK/job.json`.
2. **Local sources.** Copy camera and mic into `WORK/src/` (one sequential read). Proxy: `bin/make_proxy.sh WORK/src/CAM.MP4` (never beside a render). Every script and shell sets `OB_SRC=WORK/src/CAM.MP4 OB_MASTER=WORK/master_synced.wav OB_RENDER_DIR=WORK/render/units_cache OB_TMPDIR=WORK/render/tmp OB_VBR=40`. Unset, the engines render another video's footage and write temp to the T7.
3. **Brief and style.** Write `WORK/BRIEF.md` and `WORK/STYLE.md`.
   - Evidence: `python3 -I ~/ob-youtube/retention_study.py summary` (his saved curves) and the reference's most-replayed graph: `yt-dlp --skip-download --print "%(heatmap)j" URL`.
   - Decode the reference by behaviour: `/watch` frame sheets plus `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/overlay_freq.py REF.mp4 WORK/qc/ref_freq.json` (clean, text and cut shares, overlays a minute, median length), cuts a minute, median shot, words a minute, time to first payoff, face share, card types. The numbers go in STYLE.md and the build targets them; "more" or "fewer" is always a measured rate.
   - Promise: title and thumbnail are locked first (`/youtube`). BRIEF.md records the opening type, the second the title's subject is named, the second of the first payoff, and the one opening change this upload tests.
   - `WORK/qc/clearance.csv`: a row per client photo, clip, song and study (asset, source, consent, viewed, licence or PMID).
4. **Sync and transcribe.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sync_av.py CAM.MP4 MIC.wav WORK/synced.mov --measure-only`, then build `WORK/master_synced.wav` as the copied cut script does; when the recorder paused mid-take use `bin/sync_steps.py`. Run the copied `prep.sh`: `whisper-cli` medium.en with word times (`-ml 1 -sow -dtw medium.en -oj`), base.en for restarts, `bin/speech_words words`; each pickup alone. → `WORK/master_synced.wav`, `WORK/story/words_dtw.json`, `WORK/qc/apple_words_list.json`.
5. **Story edit.** `story/story_edit.py` writes the keep list in play order, each edge in the middle of a real gap, found by phrase. → `WORK/story/keeps_A.json`.
   - Lines he shot stay in. Remove only what he marked ("cut that, Claude": drop the call and the try before it, keep the redo) and self-corrections (keep the corrected one). Never cut a claim.
   - Nothing twice: one call to action; no roadmap ("here's the plan", a list of questions), definition or hedge in the open. The last clean take stays.
6. **Cut.** The cut script runs `cutlist.py` (`--min-pause 0.6 --keep-before 0.12 --keep-after 0.12 --soft-db -50`) with everything outside the keeps removed. → `WORK/cutlist.json`.
   - **Every shot `framing: "wide"`.** `cutlist.py` alternates wide and a 120% punch, a 20% jump OB banned. Set all to wide, delete `punch_at`, check `grep -c punch cutlist.json` prints 0. A hard-cut step within the route's scale limit is allowed only as a segment `pre_vf` crop, on a jump cut inside a stretch of him alone over 5 s, never twice within 5 s, never animated.
   - Air after his last sound follows the word before it: mid-sentence 0.09 s, comma 0.16 s, sentence end 0.24 s, only shortened. Pauses of 0.22 s and more inside a shot are cut the same way, removing only near-silence.
   - Breaths: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/breaths.py WORK` (docstring), cut like pauses keeping about 0.1 s of air.
   - Snap every shot to the render's frame grid (shot = fr(out) minus fr(in)); a continuous-time list drifts.
7. **Prove nothing is cut off or lost.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/cut_audit.py WORK`; `react_qc.py WORK build`, whisper on `qc/edit_mic16.wav`, `react_qc.py WORK compare`; `story/verify_cut.py`. → `WORK/qc/wordqc.json`, `WORK/qc/removals.json`.
8. **Lips.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/lag_curve.py WORK/qc/cam16k.wav WORK/master_synced.wav WORK/qc/lag_curve.json`; per-shot `audio_shift_ms` from the curve's lower envelope. → `WORK/qc/lag_curve.json`.
9. **Look-downs.** Once, on the 4K raw take, before graphics and beside nothing heavy: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/gaze_raw.py WORK/src/CAM.MP4 WORK/gaze/raw.json` . Print his eyes-open median and set `lookaway_check.py --th` from it; the default line flags normal looks on a different set. Eye-check the longest runs on 3-frame strips; blinks and laughs go in `story/gaze_exempt.json`. Every visible look-down of 0.3 s and longer is cut out in a pause or covered by footage over the whole phrase (`story/glance_covers.py`). → `WORK/gaze/raw.json`.
10. **Graphics plan.** The copied `make_spec.py` writes `events_spec.json` (anchors on his words, numbers as digits); then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/resolve_events.py WORK`. → `WORK/events_resolved.json`. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/qc_assets.py build WORK` and fill every row it lists (`subjects/graphics.md` section 8: which plan it reads, which fields); `viewed: true` only after you opened the sheet, saved as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. `qc_assets.py check WORK --route long-form` shows no FAIL. → `WORK/qc/assets.json`.
    - Every overlay is footage, a real photo, a chart, a drawn object or 3D, and moves. No text-only card, no still that holds. His lines go on his face (face quotes, caption plates). A vertical clip goes beside him, OB slid aside. Full-frame cards go through `bin/stage.py` (blurred moving fill, never flat black).
    - He is in every overlay while he talks (M87, OB 11-11 "im not in the overlay"): no bench or stock alone.
    - Every claim gets the thing itself: the study's real number (PubMed or USDA first; `doc_page.py` for real pages), the product's own package, the person's photo or clip. Look at every photo and clip first (`subjects/graphics.md`); cut-outs are BiRefNet only. B-roll is the picture of the line: his own footage first (`/Volumes/T7/OB-EDITS/broll-frameio-2026-10-29/CATALOG.md`), real stock only when it does not fit, none twice.
    - A clip with its own sound finishes the sentence it starts. A clip he reacts to plays whole and untouched, found by sound (`routes/reaction.md` steps 2 and 4), with only him over it. Their audio never plays under his voice.
    - Face between overlays: after every one or two, 2 s and more of him; never more than three full-frame overlays in a row; no face-only stretch over about 6 s without something of what he says. Overlay rate matches STYLE.md. No cover under about 1.5 s between two face shots, no face sliver under about 1.5 s between two covers.
    - Titles never cross a face, his or a stock person's (`card_faces.py windows` picks the clip window).
11. **Place and fit.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/head_clear.py WORK --write`; once mocks exist `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/fit_check.py WORK --write` (without it cards render at x1.00 with space round them). View every mock at full size and each animation's frames including t = 0 (`node bin/frames_at.js`). → `WORK/story/fit.json`, `WORK/qc/mock_sheet_*.jpg`.
12. **Music.** Method in `subjects/audio.md`. `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/music_pick.py WORK --route long-form --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav` on the cut's own voice, correct `audio/edit_profile.json`, apply, use. Lane `youtube-safe`, credited in the description. One track carries the video; a second joins only at a chapter card. The engine takes the cue sheet through `OB_SCORE` (`bin/score.py` docstring); build it from `audio/manifest.json`. Measure each chapter's bed against a stereo copy of the voice before rendering. → `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`.
13. **Sound effects.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sfx_pick.py WORK --route long-form`: bank sounds on the entrances that need one (a swipe into a clip with sound, a click on montage cuts, a shutter on photo cards, a paper slide on documents, a soft rise into a chapter), at the route's cap. Absolute paths, as `OB_SFX_PLAN`. → `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json`.
14. **Breakdown and fresh check (no hold).** Write `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`: the structure on one page (cut, moved, why), each shot and overlay with time, source and the line it answers, the music pick with runner-ups, the sound plan, flags as one clause each, and the contact sheets (a frame every 2 s with the graphic id; close-ups of every client, photo and clip). Give BRIEF, STYLE, BREAKDOWN and the sheets to the `work-checker` agent and fix what it flags. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/roughcut.py WORK WORK/src/CAM.proxy.mp4 WORK/preview/rough.mp4` and check the flow on frames. → `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`.
15. **Precheck.** `bin/precheck.sh WORK --keep-going`. Rerun until all five pass. Never pass by `OB_FIT_MODE=stored`, `OB_PACE_ARGS` or `OB_FLICKER_ALLOW`; a real gag goes in `story/flicker_allow.json` with the event id and why.
16. **Render.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/render_slots.py list`. Detached: `HYPERFRAMES_RENDER_DETACHED=1 OB_SRC=... OB_MASTER=... OB_SCORE=... OB_SFX_PLAN=... OB_VBR=40 OB_RENDER_DIR=... OB_TMPDIR=... nohup perl -MPOSIX -e 'POSIX::setsid(); exec @ARGV' caffeinate -i python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/assemble.py WORK WORK/render/OUT.mp4 --keep`. Render the intro first (`--range 0 170`), look at its frames, then the full file. The engine has no gate of its own.
17. **Check the real file.**
    - `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/render_review.py WORK WORK/render/OUT.mp4 --src WORK/src/CAM.MP4` (no `--phone`): frames, black, loudness, lips per shot, a sheet every 2 s. Look at them.
    - `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/overlay_touch.py WORK` and `card_faces.py rendered WORK ID ...`: no graphic on a face.
    - `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/overlay_freq.py WORK/render/OUT.mp4 WORK/qc/out_freq.json` against STYLE.md.
    - `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py gate WORK WORK/render/OUT.mp4`. It builds the pieces from the cut list, so the cut-off-word check runs on the whole file. A SKIP on `edges` is a FAIL: supply what it names and rerun.
    - Transcribe the finished audio; no "cut that, Claude" survives.
18. **Review and finish.** `video-reviewer` gets the file, BRIEF, BREAKDOWN and the exemplar numbers, against `~/ob-editor/qc/REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md`. Three rounds at most. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py finish WORK WORK/render/OUT.mp4 --to "C#### title"`. One 4K file.
19. **Upload notes** in `WORK/UPLOAD-NOTES.md`, copied into the delivery note: title and thumbnail from `/youtube`; description with chapters (`0:00 Intro` plus each chapter card), study DOIs, music credit; default tags plus 15 to 20 topic tags; an AFTER PUBLISH block with the end-screen window (his CTA line to the end) and one card per own video he names, on the line it extends (teaser in his first person, 30 characters at most). CTA link `https://obhealthfit.co/book` with `utm_source=youtube`. Posting, the A/B test and the Studio session are his word only.

## Decisions that are yours
- **Breakdown:** no hold; step 14.
- **Risky lines** (sponsor, guarantee, dose, medical advice) stay in, no graphic. His number against the receipts is one clause in the delivery note; a card shows the series figure or no number.
- **Opening:** his face is frame 1; nothing full-screen covers him before 3 s; hook words sit on his face as caption plates. No opening type is proven: pick the one that fits and say what the video gives the viewer, in concrete words, inside the first minute. Do not cut a credential line or "welcome back" to fix a curve.
- **Clients:** per `subjects/proof-clients.md`, a consented before/after within a second of every clients, "over a decade" or client-name line; a fast flash on "helped thousands"; the close is a client run, each finishing its sentence. No credential line is added to the open; one he said carries the proof. Never brief "no client photos".
- **Music and stock:** the editor picks music from scored candidates, runner-ups in the note. Stock is only the picture of the line, real, never AI.
- **Beat notes after delivery** ("beat N: ..."): `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/beats.py redo longform:KEY N [M ...]` (keys in `bin/beats_adapters/longform_projects.json`). Never re-render the whole video for a beat note; gate and reviewer rerun.

No recorded pieces? `subjects/qc.md`, "When the gate says there are no recorded pieces". `WORK/pieces.json` (look-away check, needs the job's camera) is `[[start s, end s], ...]` in the camera file's time, one per kept piece: the cut list's `src_in`/`src_out` when the master is synced to the camera (the gate cannot build it safely: the mic and camera clocks differ by a sync offset).
- Records in WORK: `audio/music_candidates.json`, `story/sfx_plan.json`, the asset record (every photo and clip viewed), `BREAKDOWN.md` with the checker's pass, `qc/warn_answers.json`.
- Delivery note: file, gate table, music pick and runner-ups, chapters, flags as clauses, upload notes. Log in `~/ob-editor/STATE-youtube.md`. Day 2 and day 7 reads: `subjects/after-posting.md`.
