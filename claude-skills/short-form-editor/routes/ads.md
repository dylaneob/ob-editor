# Route: Ads and boosted posts  (`ad` in formats.json)

Use it when: OB asks for an ad, a boosted post, or product UGC (EkkoVision, Trybe brands, TikTok Shop), or hands over film-day takes for an ad, or a reference ad to copy. Scripts, hooks, film sheets, casting and results live in `/ad-maker` (coaching DM ads on Meta) and `/ugc` (product ads); this file is the cut. Also read: `subjects/audio.md`, `subjects/graphics.md`, `subjects/proof-clients.md` (coaching ads carry clients), `subjects/qc.md`, `subjects/delivery.md`. Reviewer list: `~/ob-editor/qc/REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md`.

## The bar
- **Product UGC cut method:** EkkoVision MASTER1 v4 (OB 10-27: "its amazing"; kept every line he recorded, last take only, dead space cut with edges and never tempo: "i meant with cuts"). Reply-to-comment intro: Rythm YR12 (OB 11-06: "way better"). Measured numbers: `~/ob-editor/qc/gold.json` (format `ad`), `GOLD-SET.md` section 2f. Reference ads (Haus) run about 5% dead space with a median dead run of 0.07 to 0.08 s.
- **Coaching DM ads:** no approved cut yet. The look is the 11-10 workshop batch spec: `/Volumes/OB-ARCHIVE/AD-MAKER/batches/2026-11-10-coaching-workshop-ads/STYLE-SPEC.md` (Instagram-native overlay text with no bubble cards, dead space cut, women-only photos accurate to each line, two cuts per ad: the 2x2 green-screen grid and a talking version). Treat the first as a test; the whole gate and a reviewer still apply.
- **A good ad:** a hook that is not generic, a product or proof on screen by about 2 s, the body in modules so a batch does not sound like one video, b-roll that is the picture of the words, the offer, the CTA.

## Inputs
1. The approved script / film sheet (`FILM-SHEET.md` in the batch folder under `/Volumes/OB-ARCHIVE/AD-MAKER/batches/`): hooks, bodies, CTAs, the combinations to cut, the keyword, `music: bed|none`. A copy of a reference ad keeps every line, word for word, in his last takes (OB 10-27: cutting it to 4 lines "was horrible").
2. RAW footage only (a captioned export bleeds the old captions): camera files and the mic WAV. A creator's phone or FX3 shoot: ingest first (below).
3. Product UGC: the reference ad file from `<OB's private archive>`, the brand's product photos, and a release from anyone else on camera.
4. Coaching ads: the consented clients for the script's persona from `subjects/proof-clients.md`; photos only from the approved proof library.

## Steps
1. **Start.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py start ad WORK --camera CAM.MP4 --mic MIC.wav --script SCRIPT.md`. If the film sheet says no music, add `--music none --music-none-why "film sheet: no music"` (and `--sfx none` only when it says no sound effects). Read what it prints; it carries the ad safe zone and the sound-effect cap. → `WORK/job.json`.
2. **Ingest the takes.** Group every repeated try of the same line and keep only the last clean one: `takes.py`. Word times from Apple's speech on the mic (`WORK/words.json`). For a creator's multi-take shoot use the Rythm ingest (project copy, read only: copy into WORK), in the folder `rythm ads (client R)/_work/scripts/` on `/Volumes/OB-ARCHIVE`: `ingest.py all`, which splits OB's voice from the creator's by pitch and level, then pick the best take per line from the transcript. **Best take = right words and eyes at the camera** (OB 11-06: "she looked at camera for the good one"): open a frame of every candidate. **Reference audio leaks:** he plays the reference ad from his phone between lines (OB 10-27: "u kept the guy i was copying ... its the other guys voice"). For every used line check its level against the take's median (the reference sits 15 to 25 dB under his voice) and that he is on screen; for a two-speaker utterance (a dash in the transcript) split it first. After the render scan the audio for voiced runs under 150 Hz that are not his (her voice is about 300 Hz, his about 100). → `WORK/plan/takes.json` listing each line, the take chosen and the frame you checked.
3. **Cut.** `zero_dead.cut_words` with edges from each line's own level (cut dead runs of 70 ms and more inside lines, round edges to the frame, edges hug the words and sit on the line's own level); never a speed-up; cut breaths like pauses (keep a little release and onset so he is never cut off); no piece under 0.30 s within 0.25 s of a neighbour (rejoin it) and none under 0.30 s alone (drop it): the "glitchy ... .5 seconds of either nothing or weird" scrap rule (OB 11-06). The method note: vault `90-References/ZERO-DEAD-SPACE-CUTS-2026-10-27.md`. Measure the word gaps on the render with Apple's words, not only silence. The UGC cutter that proved this is `ekko.py` (project copy, read only: `/Volumes/OB-ARCHIVE/ekko ads/_work/scripts/ekko.py`); for a new creator copy `ads.py` and `rythm.py` from the Rythm folder above. Coaching ads: `tmpl.py` (see `routes/templates.md` for the plan and the matte rule) with the element `igtext` from `~/ob-editor/bin/tmpl_ig.py`. → `WORK/plan/edl.json`.
4. **Voice.** The mic through `voice_master.py`, level-matched so the seated voiceover is as loud as the on-camera takes. Check lip sync with `lipsync_check.py` before trusting a sync file.
5. **Build the variations from the film sheet.** Cut only the hook x body x CTA combinations the film sheet names, every hook on the best body first, then body and CTA swaps on the top hooks. Every hook usable on its own gets its own ad on a proven body; a hook with the product working (the device on her arm filling while she talks) is an ad with no montage at all. Bodies must differ (OB 11-06: "the body is legit the same on all of them ... every video sounds the same"): build bodies from modules (full, short, 3-step, talking montage, none) with different line sets, and re-scan all of her lines for unused takes before writing. Read each finished script start to finish as a viewer: cut any opener that needs missing context, bridge the hook into the process, name the brand in the offer when the CTA says "they", give every claim its reason on screen if it was not said. Product continuity: the device on her arm never appears or vanishes between shots (tag beats pre, process, on, off; only forward, except the device-on hook then application flashback). → `WORK/plan/combos.json`, one row per file with its hook, body, CTA and name `S###-ANGLE-FMT-V##_KEYWORD_YYMMDD` (the Meta ad name is the keyword).
6. **Pictures: every one is the picture of its line.** B-roll for "coverage in all the right places" is fingers on the upper chest, not a stranger (OB 10-28: "make sure im actually lifting in the clip not just setting up", "why are you randomly showing client R"). Twin splits use settled poses only. Watch every candidate clip and photo; reject bent-over or setup moments (OB 11-09: "I hate this shot") and every banned file (C1797 to C1802; his lean photo is DSC07329). Real stock clips are allowed where the sentence needs a real person or object (ages spread across 25 to 34, 35 to 44 and 45 to 60 for a women 25+ audience, OB 11-10): `~/ob-editor/bin/stock_pexels.py` and `stock_mixkit.py`, credited in `WORK/PHOTO-CREDITS.txt`. No AI image, voice, avatar or music anywhere in an ad. The deal background is the product grid with the original price struck through, no discount code (OB 10-27). Save a contact sheet of every chosen picture as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/qc_assets.py build WORK` and fill every row it lists (`subjects/graphics.md` section 8: which plan it reads, which fields); `viewed: true` only after you opened the sheet, saved as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. `qc_assets.py check WORK --route ad` shows no FAIL. → `WORK/qc/assets.json` and the sheets, listed in `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`.
7. **Cutouts and green screen: BiRefNet only.** `~/OpenMontage/.venv/bin/python -I ~/ob-editor/bin/matte_hq.py IN.mp4 OUT_DIR FPS`. Never blur a box beside him ("You put blur next to me", OB 11-09), never erase or blur board writing he wrote ("I did that to increase retention" was the wrong reason), no rim or ghost hands. Look at 6 matted frames over the real background. → `WORK/qc/matte-sheet.jpg`. The `tmpl.py` matte step needs the BiRefNet replacement in `routes/templates.md` step 7.
8. **Grade by measurement** (OB 11-09: "I hate your color grading honestly, no color at all it seems", "Made it darker too"). His FX3 footage is flat. Measure saturation, skin saturation and the 5th and 95th luma percentiles on his frames and on the reference ads, then grade to the reference: the v7 grade in `~/ob-scratch/ekko-1109/edit/engine.py` (`make_cube7`, `grade7`, `gym7`) does it per lighting segment with three anchors (darkest 1%, cheek skin, brightest 1%): skin never darker, whites at most slightly lifted, midtone S-curve. Put a before/after frame sheet of each lighting setup in `WORK/qc/grade-sheet.jpg`. Never lift the highlights on his face.
9. **Text and captions.** Captions in the one brand font (Big Shoulders), #FFD400 on the key word, no bubble cards, no boxes (Instagram-native text via `igtext` in coaching ads). Coaching ads: a two-line header from 0.0 s (callout, promise) and the keyword strip by 2 s held to the end. All text inside the **ad safe zone** printed by `ob_job.py start` (stricter than the reel zone; formats.json `text.ad_safe_*`), never over his face, no "you" statements aimed at the viewer's body, and the banned words and dashes in `subjects/graphics.md`. Hook within the route's word limit. Check frame 0 for pop-in.
10. **Zoom.** Coaching ads: none. Product UGC only: a hard cut to a tighter crop between two words near the middle of a line of about 1.9 s or longer, the sound untouched, never animated, never on the offer or price frame (the kit must show), and inside the route's hard-cut scale limit.
11. **Music and sound.** If the film sheet says bed: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/music_pick.py WORK --route ad --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav` then `music_pick.py apply WORK`. The lane is royalty-free only (licensed or original audio only on paid ads); the route's bed level and the upbeat rule for teaching content apply. Sound effects: write `WORK/plan/moments.json`, then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sfx_pick.py WORK --route ad`, at the route's cap. See `subjects/audio.md`. → `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`, `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json` (or the declared none).
12. **Breakdown, written and checked.** `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`: the script with the takes chosen, the combos, the stills (`tmpl.py still PLAN OUT.jpg T1 T2 ...`, or one frame sheet), asset sheet, matte sheet, grade sheet. A fresh `work-checker` agent reads it against the script before the full render. The first render of a new ad look is one frame sheet through that same check. Nobody waits for OB.
13. **Render** (`render_slots.py list` first), then **diff the render against the script**: transcribe the cut with whisper large-v3-turbo and Apple's speech, and compare word by word; every line he recorded and every line of a copy must be present (OB: "never clip words"). Run a stutter check for repeated frames. → `WORK/qc/script-diff.txt`.
14. **Look at the real output:** 10 fps contact sheet of each file in `WORK/qc/final-*.jpg`, opened in full. Check the beginning (no dead 0.5 s of room noise after a line), the cuts (no scraps), the match of picture to words, the cutout edges, text clear of the zone edges and his face, colour and skin. Fix and re-render.

## Decisions that are yours
- **Music: bed or none.** The film sheet's `music` field decides; absent means a bed (OB 11-06: every edit gets a music bed). The reference ad's own music does not decide.
- **Which look for a coaching ad.** The STYLE-SPEC's: a talking cut with him on screen every frame (OB 11-10: "For the talking head one never not show me, do overlays on screen instead": photos, study cards and clients sit on his chest under his chin as overlay cards, never cutaways) and a 2x2 green-screen grid (T09) cut. Both, unless the film sheet names one.
- **Which clients.** Per the persona and `subjects/proof-clients.md`; women-only photos for a women's ad, accurate to each line.
- **Hook candidates.** Inventory every hook take (scripted and improvised) before choosing; each usable hook is an ad.
- **A line he never said on camera** (check who said it by pitch and level): it goes on screen as a text hook, not as voice.
- **Name and files.** One file per ad; no extra copies of any kind and no trial pack. Files are delivered by `ob_job.py finish`; OB submits to Trybe himself.

## Traps
- "the body is legit the same on all of them" (11-06), "cutting it to 4 lines was horrible" (10-27), "you have me talking not her at the start" (11-07): steps 2, 5.
- "pacing seems slow compared to the best, too much deadspace" (11-07): the gap was inhales between lines; step 3 cuts breaths.
- "I hate your color grading" and "You put blur next to me" (11-09): steps 7, 8.
- "never not show me" on the workshop ads (11-10): the talking cut keeps him on screen; step 9 and the final sheet.
- The gate cannot see a good ad. The script diff and the look at the final sheet are yours.

## Finish
- Gate inputs: the script, recorded pieces and camera map, `assets` record, `warn_answers.json` per WARN, music and sfx records when used. `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py status WORK` lists what is missing.
- `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py gate WORK FILE`, then a fresh `video-reviewer` on each file with the script, `BREAKDOWN.md`, the sheets and the exemplar for the format. Three rounds at most.
- `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py finish WORK FILE --to "ad <slug>"` for each file.
- Delivery note: the combos delivered with their names, the music pick and runner-ups, any factual conflict as one clause. Update the registry row per ad (`/ad-maker` registry; `/ugc` `UGC-REGISTRY.csv`). Launch, budgets and Meta changes are OB's word only.
