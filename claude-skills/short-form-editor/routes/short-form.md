# Route: Reel from raw talking takes  (`reel-talking` in formats.json)

Use it when: OB sends raw talking takes (camera file plus mic WAV, usually with a script) and says "edit this" or "make this a reel" with no template named. A named creator or T-number goes to `routes/templates.md`; lists to `routes/lists.md`. Also read: `subjects/audio.md`, `subjects/graphics.md`, `subjects/proof-clients.md`, `subjects/qc.md`, `subjects/delivery.md`.

## The bar
- **Approved exemplars.** For the cut: the 10/10 feed re-cuts (`OB-1010-HORMONES-FEED-VO.mp4`, `OB-1010-MICROS-FEED-VO.mp4`), OB 10-29: "wow so much better". For the graphics level: the GLP level test, OB 11-06: "That's what I'm fucking talking about let's go, way better". Both are listed in `~/ob-editor/qc/gold.json`; the reviewer compares your file to them.
- **Measured shape of the approved files:** -14 LUFS, silence at or under 3%, no silence over 0.2 s, lip sync inside 10 ms, something moving on screen early (the 10/10 feeds first moved at 2.3 to 3.0 s and were flagged; do better: first move inside 1 s). Graphics beat: 40 or more events a minute (the 10/10 feeds ran 39 to 45; the GLP level ran 75 to 85). Pick the 10/10 rate when the script is plain talk and the GLP rate when the script names objects, numbers and places on nearly every line.
- **Creator reference:** none. A named creator makes it `reel-graphics`.
- **Openings** (his skip data on 6,130 reels, `~/ob-editor/presets/PROVEN-OPENERS.md`): text on screen from frame 1 with 3 or more readable words by 1.6 s; the largest face under 8% of the frame at 0.4 s (a medium shot, not a close-up); nothing louder than about -15 dBFS in the first 0.5 s; for food, the number on screen in frame 1. Frame 1 is him talking, not a card (M35, OB 10-30: "my face is alwasy in the opener").

## Inputs
- Camera file `C####.MP4` and the mic: the MOTIV recording `MainOutput_24bit.wav` in a `My_Activity_<date>_<time>` folder on the T7 (reading the T7 is fine). Pair them by length (the WAV is about as long as the video). If only a 32-bit file exists, run the 24-bit fix first (tools list: `subjects/tools.md`).
- The script, one line per line, with the hook lines marked. No script: `speech_words` the take and write the script file from the transcript, one sentence per line, as he said it.
- Raw, uncaptioned footage only. A file with burned-in captions is not an input: find the raw camera file on the T7; if none exists, stop and say so.
- A reference video, if he sent one, decoded before the build (`/watch`; `/rea-video` when graphics must be placed). Layout and timing come from the decode; fonts and colours stay the brand's.

## Steps
Start: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py start reel-talking WORK --camera CAM.MP4 --mic MIC.wav --script SCRIPT` and read what it prints (levels, caps, required checks). Everything below lands in WORK.

1. **Sync.** `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sync_av.py CAM.MP4 MIC.wav --measure-only` prints offset and drift; it refuses a residual over 15 ms (wrong take). Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sync_av.py CAM.MP4 MIC.wav --audio-only WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav`. If the take was recorded while a render ran, the offset is a staircase: use `sync_steps.py` instead (`--help` for arguments). The video is graded later by the plan's `lut`; the default is `~/ob-editor/luts/ob_grade_warmtan_hald.png` (warm tan: face brought down, skin colour kept, highlights not lifted). Never `REJECTED_ob_grade_A_hald.png` (made him look albino). Grade by measurement: skin hue and level on three frames of the contact sheet in step 9 against the camera's own frame; never lift highlights on his face. → `WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav`, the offset line in `WORK/notes.md`.
2. **Words.** `~/ob-editor/bin/speech_words words WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav > WORK/words.json`. Save the script as `WORK/script.txt`. → `WORK/words.json`.
3. **Bad takes out, zero dead space, clean voice**, one command:
   `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/takes.py WORK/words.json WORK/script.txt --audio WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav --fps 29.97 --edl WORK/edl.json --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav --master`
   - It finds every try of every script line, drops a try that "cut that, Claude" follows, one that stops short, and a restart, and keeps the last clean try. A line with no clean try keeps its most complete last try and is marked REVIEW: read the drop list and every REVIEW line against the audio, and pick the complete one. Lines he shot stay in; only a try he cued away is dropped (OB 11-09: "just do what i tell you").
   - Each kept take is cut by `zero_dead.cut_words`: starts on the first word's onset, ends when the last word's sound goes down (soft endings like the "you" of "Not for you" stay), every pause of 70 ms or more between words is out, nothing inside a word is cut, never a tempo change. Inhales sit in those pauses and go with them. OB 10-28: "not speed it up but just cut absoleultely all deadspac".
   - The voice comes from the MOTIV mic through `voice_master`, never camera audio and never a 16 kHz copy; every line levelled to the median line, loudest syllables only compressed, then the master loudness with a 4x limiter, never a hard clip. Detail: `subjects/audio.md`.
   → `WORK/edl.json`, `WORK/audio/voice.wav`.
4. **Prove the cut before anything is drawn.** All four must pass, each saved to WORK:
   - `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/takes.py WORK/words.json WORK/script.txt --audio WORK/audio/mic_synced.wav --fps 29.97 --edl WORK/edl.json --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav --verify > WORK/qc/verify.txt`: whisper medium word by word against the script, 0 missing or clipped words. Apple's words (`speech_words words WORK/audio/voice.wav`) are the second reading: a word lost in one reading and present in the other is played and judged by ear.
   - `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/zero_dead.py measure WORK/audio/voice.wav > WORK/qc/measure.txt`: silence and the longest gap inside the route's limits.
   - `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/voice_master.py check WORK/audio/voice.wav > WORK/qc/voice_check.txt`: no flat-topped peaks, loudness and true peak inside the master limits.
   - Write the pieces file the gate reads, a list of [start, end] pairs in source seconds: `python3 -I -c "import json;d=json.load(open('WORK/edl.json'));json.dump([p for l in d['lines'] for p in l['pieces']],open('WORK/pieces.json','w'))"`. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/zero_dead.py lookoff CAM.MP4 WORK/pieces.json > WORK/qc/lookoff.txt`: no piece opens or closes on him looking down or away (3 frames per edge, 2 must agree). Fix a flag in this order: another clean take of that line; a cut-away over that edge (his own footage that shows the sentence, viewed first); the next real pause for the edge. Never by cutting into a word, and never by "listing it for OB".
   Every edge also has to sit between words on a real pause proven against the source audio (the gate's `edges` check): the edge snaps to a whole word and a whole sentence, not mid-sentence (`moves.snap(audio, start, end)`; OB 10-28: "you still cut off on the fdoe"). Words said in one breath ("615 calories. I love") join on the quietest point between them, never repeated or clipped. Mocks and previews are cut from the raw take, never from a posted render (that inherited the old cut-offs, M27).
5. **Captions text.** Every word he says is captioned, in his words (the gate warns when captions differ from speech; each WARN gets an answer in `WORK/qc/warn_answers.json`). A word whose midpoint falls in a cut is placed by its end time, then its start; it is never dropped. Three words at a time, the live word yellow, below his face on the shirt, never over his mouth. Brand type: `~/ob-editor/brand/BRAND.md`.
6. **Hook.** Locked before drawing: the script's hook line, shown as text from frame 1 in his own words, beside or below his face, clear of the route's safe bands. A reference's hook shape may be copied; its words may not. → one line `hook:` at the top of `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`.
7. **Graphics plan.** One entry per overlay (it becomes an `els` entry of the step 9 plan): time, spoken word it lands on, `kind` (footage, real photo, chart, drawn object, 3D), the media file, its source, `viewed: true`. Rules in `subjects/graphics.md`; the ones that bite here: he is on screen every frame he talks, graphics go beside him or on his chest; no text-only card; nothing over his face; no black or near-black panel; the picture is the sentence (a green-screen background or b-roll shows the thing he names, never random stock); cutouts are BiRefNet only (`~/OpenMontage/.venv/bin/python -I ~/ob-editor/bin/matte_hq.py IN.mp4 OUT_DIR FPS`), never Apple person-segmentation masks, GrabCut or drawn masks; his own photos only from the banked retouched set, rotated, never C1797 to C1802, his lean photo is DSC07329. Every photo and clip goes on a contact sheet first: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/frame_sheet.py MEDIA WORK/qc/sheets/ROW_ID.jpg T T T ...` (ROW_ID is the overlay's id in the plan), and `BREAKDOWN.md` lists each sheet. A source with no verified licence and no real-photo origin (AI stock sites, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Midjourney) is out.
8. **Proof.** If any line says clients, coaching, "over a decade" or names a client, follow `subjects/proof-clients.md` (section 6: the events file the `clients` check reads): a consent-screened client before/after on screen within a second of the words, in the intro proof line and the CTA too.
9. **Plan, stills, breakdown.** Write the `tmpl.py` plan (`python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/tmpl.py --help` prints the schema; elements come from `tmpl_fx.py`): `cam`, `mic`, `lut`, `timeline` built from `WORK/edl.json` pieces, `els` from the graphics plan, `captions` {"y": 1180, "n": 3}, `sfx` from step 11. `tmpl.py` is this route's engine because it records the pieces and runs the gate. Every look may go through `tmpl.py`: it cuts OB out with BiRefNet itself, cached, and writes `WORK/qc/matte.json` (`routes/templates.md` step 7). `ob-clipper` and OpenMontage `silence_cutter` are not used. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/tmpl.py still WORK/plan.json WORK/stills.jpg 0.1 T T T` for frame 1, each graphic's landing, and the last second, and write `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`: per shot, time, his line, picture and source, overlay kind and file, sound-effect event, music section. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/qc_assets.py build WORK` and fill every row it lists (`subjects/graphics.md` section 8: which plan it reads, which fields); `viewed: true` only after you opened the sheet, saved as `WORK/qc/sheets/<row id>.jpg`. `qc_assets.py check WORK --route reel-talking` shows no FAIL. → `WORK/plan.json`, `WORK/stills.jpg`, `WORK/BREAKDOWN.md`, `WORK/qc/assets.json`. A fresh `work-checker` agent reads `BREAKDOWN.md`, the script and the stills before the full render and must answer PASS (fix, re-check, three rounds). This replaces any approval step: OB is not asked.
10. **Music.** `subjects/audio.md`: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/music_pick.py WORK --route reel-talking --voice WORK/audio/voice.wav` scores at least three candidates against this cut, then `music_pick.py apply WORK` mixes the pick. Upbeat and bright for teaching, never moody (OB 11-07: "You always go so depressing"). → `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`, `WORK/audio/manifest.json`.
11. **Sound effects.** Write `WORK/plan/moments.json` (`[{"id", "t", "what"}]`, one per graphic that lands), then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/sfx_pick.py WORK --route reel-talking`: from the bank, each hit tied to an on-screen event, quiet, under the route's cap, none in the first 0.5 s. OB 10-29: "use them when needed dont be obxious about them". → `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json`. With no hard landing in the picture, write no plan (sound effects are optional on this route) and say so in `notes.md`.
12. **Render** after `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/render_slots.py list` shows a free slot: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/tmpl.py render WORK/plan.json WORK/out/render.mp4` (the gate runs by itself). Mix: `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/music_pick.py mix WORK WORK/out/render.mp4`; the gate and reviewer run on its `_mix.mp4`.
13. **Look at the real output.** Contact sheet of the render every 0.5 s of the first 3 s and then every 2 s: `frame_sheet.py WORK/out/render.mp4 WORK/qc/final_sheet.jpg T T ...`. Compare each frame with the stills and the graphics plan. Check lips against the sound on the first, middle and last piece. Fix and re-render before the gate.
14. **Gate, reviewer, finish:** see Finish.

## Decisions that are yours
- **Renderer:** `tmpl.py` (step 9). HyperFrames only for an element `tmpl_fx` cannot draw, and then through `subjects/graphics.md`.
- **Graphics rate:** by the script (bar, above). With a reference reel, match its measured overlays per minute from the decode (25 to 30 of its reels at 10 fps), never a feeling of "more" or "fewer" (OB 10-21, 10-29 and 11-04 swung both ways until a number was pinned).
- **Grade:** warm tan LUT, no menu of three.
- **Which take of a line:** the last clean take; REVIEW lines get the most complete one. Which of two equally clean takes: the one where his eyes are on the lens at both edges.
- **A factual conflict in the script** (a wrong number he said): the line stays, one clause in the delivery note.
- **Bed or none:** a bed on every edit (the route says `bed`); the voice stem stays in WORK so the bed swaps in a minute.
- **Zoom:** none. A hard cut that changes scale within the route's limit is allowed only to hide a join that has no other cover.

## Traps
- **Cut off mid-word** (OB 10-25: "you cut me off mid word ... Never do that shit again"; 10-28 twice more). Stopped by step 4 (`--verify`, the gate's `edges` and `words`) and the snap rule.
- **Bad takes left in** (10-25: "you don't take out bad takes"; the cue words vanish when a cleaned transcript is used). Run `takes.py` on the raw words, never a cleaned transcript.
- **Looking away or down at an edge** (10-19 to 10-29, four times). Step 4 `lookoff`; the gate fails it without `WORK/pieces.json` and the job's camera, so write both (step 4).
- **Zooming** (OB 11-07: "its zooming in and out non stop and looks retarded"). Old presets that alternate 100% and 108-115% are dead.
- **Fat photo as lean** (OB 11-07: "y do you always show me fat asf as toned and shredded so odd"). Contact sheet, banned set, DSC07329.
- **Music too loud or sad** (10-20, 10-30, 11-07). `music_pick.py` and the route's bed level.
- **Captions not what he said**. Step 5 and `warn_answers.json`.

## Finish
Gate inputs for this route: the camera file, the mic, the script, `WORK/pieces.json` (look-off), `WORK/audio/music_candidates.json`, `WORK/story/sfx_plan.json` (when sound effects are used), `WORK/qc/assets.json`, `warn_answers.json`. Run `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py gate WORK WORK/out/render.mp4`; a check that cannot run is a FAIL. Hand the `video-reviewer` agent the file path, the script, `BREAKDOWN.md` and the gold exemplar names; fix what it flags, three rounds at most. Then `python3 -I ~/ob-editor/bin/ob_job.py finish WORK WORK/out/render.mp4 --to "what it is"`. The delivery note (see `subjects/delivery.md`) says: what it is, the music title with its runner-ups, any one-clause factual conflict, nothing else.
