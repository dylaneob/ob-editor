## Beat sheet
**Signature moves:**
1. **Someone else's clip opens alone** for 5.6-10 s with its own music and text; he says nothing. The music hard-cuts on the picture cut (15-35 dB drop in 100 ms) and his first word lands 0.0-0.3 s later, answering the clip. (6 of 7 voiced reels; 57% of his last 84.)
2. **He is a cut-out, never a full-frame talking head:** head and shoulders, background removed, no stroke/shadow/box, in a bottom corner (face 256-414 px wide, centre y 1215-1507) over full-frame footage that changes every 2.4-5.5 s. He picks the corner away from the action.
3. **A picture of the inside of the body for every claim:** licensed, moving 3D anatomy (Muscle & Motion, credited), often a skeleton doing the same rep as the human, stacked as a top panel (seam y 782). Lands a median **0.66 s before** its word.
4. **Proof is a named person on camera:** symptom, years of failed help, a test that brings it on, a named drill with cues and an exact dose, then a retest that closes the loop.
5. **Mark grammar on footage:** red = the fault (circle, line, arrow, X, dashed alignment curve), green = the good side or path, yellow = force or direction. 1-3 s each, on its word.
6. **No spoken ask.** The reel ends on the result line; the ask is a comment question in the post caption.

**Pace:** 8-16 background changes a minute (median 10.7), about 220 wpm (196-246), no pause over 0.45 s, live score 6.0-9.5. Motion density is not what separates his outliers: the topic, the stitch, the anatomy reveal and his face (90% face share on the two biggest plays outliers) are.
**First 2 s:** the other creator's moving clip with their text (6 of 7). The one exception opens on orbiting anatomy with a surprise-flag line and one yellow underlined keyword.
**Energy curve:** fast reply hook, a slower test/why middle, a fix ladder, a short retest payoff.
**Audio:** dry voice, no music bed and no SFX under him (measured), about -14 LUFS; music only inside the stitch.
**Captions:** optional. 3 of 7 voiced reels have none, including the two biggest plays outliers. When used: heavy condensed italic caps, 2-5 words, centred between the footage's action and his head (y 794-1133).
