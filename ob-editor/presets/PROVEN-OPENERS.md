# PROVEN OPENERS v0 (2026-10-22)

Read this before building any reel opening or trial variant. It comes from OB's own skip rate on 6,130 reels (2,214 grid and regular, 3,916 trials, Jan 2025 to Sep 2026; trials never pooled with the grid).
- Full evidence: vault `80-Performance/_SKIP-RATE-PLAYBOOK-2026-10-22.md` §10, ledger B36.
- Skip rate = the share of views gone in the first 3 seconds. Inside a month it tracks reach (-0.83), not saves or shares. It is a reach signal: never judge a saves or shares post on it.
- These are associations from his history. The Trial Lab's same-video versions are the causal test. Update this file when a Trial Lab round settles a style (median paired difference over 10 to 15 videos).

## Defaults (each held on the grid AND on trials)
1. **Two people in the first frame.** OB and the source both visible at 0.4 s. Grid -3.6 points, trials -5.9. Reach 1.20x, follows 1.11x. This is the opener OB has dropped most (75% of grid reels in 2025 Q1, 56% in 2026 Q3).
2. **No big face close-up at 0.4 s.** Open on a medium shot: the largest face under 8% of the frame. A close-up runs grid +2.1, trials +5.1, reach 0.77x, shares 0.84x.
3. **Text on screen from the first frame:** 3+ readable words by 1.6 s. Grid -3.1, trials -3.9. Saves 1.16x, shares 1.26x: the best of the set.
4. **No blast in the first half-second.** Keep the opening below about -15 dBFS RMS: no sting, scream or hard music hit at 0.0 s. Loudest third: grid +1.2, trials +2.5, reach 0.88x. This is separate from the -1 dB output limit.
5. **For food content, the number on screen in the first frame** (the calorie figure or the claim's number). Grid -2.3, trials -3.55, follows 1.18x. Not on a saves job: inside audits it costs saves (0.83x).

## Conditional
6. **On a reach or ceiling trial, let the picture carry the first 3 seconds.** Keep it to 9 or fewer spoken words, or let the source's own intro go first.
   - Fewest words: skip down, but follows 0.76x.
   - On a follows job, keep OB talking (the most-words third runs 1.26x follows) and apply rules 1 to 5 instead.
   - The source's own day-of-eating intro first is the exception: lower skip AND 1.21x follows. On audits, open on her intro.

## Tested, not rules (don't use as defaults)
- The 26 hook formulas: none beats OB's own lines. On trials, "The Statistic" (+5.4), "Insider Leak" (+3.5) and "Numbered With A Favourite" (+2.3) skip more.
- Question openings, picture motion in the first second, length and series packaging: no skip gap.
- OB's tease in the opening of an audit (claim test, verdict tease or mistake tease): no skip gap (OB's hook inside 5 s, B36.11), but breakouts 19 vs 6.81 expected (2.79x, B2.23) and, adjusted for month and length, breakouts 2.76x vs 1.65x with saves 1.33x and follows only 1.07x (B26.11). It is a ceiling lever, not a skip lever. Quote it as "a tease in the opening", never "in the first 3 seconds" (label reliability kappa 0.17, B2.30).
- Repackaging a flopped post: a remake of the same claim runs follows 0.71x (B25.7); a repost of a non-breakout breaks out at 0.33x the expected rate (B25.4); the same footage reposted skips +3.55 points more (B36.8). Fix the opening on the next NEW video.
- Teaching reels at 2 to 3 minutes: 120 s+ follows 0.84x, reach 0.62x (n=27); 90 to 119 s is the band that converted (follows 2.21x, n=31) (B30.1). Keep teaching to 119 s or move it to YouTube.
- A contrarian first line ("not", "never", "stop", "wrong", "NOT what you think"): talk-lane follows bottom quarter (B41.2), text-lane reach and saves lower (B41.7). Other creators' wins with it (Berg) stack a superlative. Don't use as a default.
